# Lega launch film: three storylines

The one thing: one button frees up a full Mac without breaking anything.
Viewer to remember: "I tap once and my Mac has room again, and the apps I have open are fine."
Spine: one Mac at 6.6 GB free travels from red to 19.6 GB free.
Facts come from the Lega app and lega.wiraperdana.com. The 6.6 GB and 19.6 GB figures are illustrative.

Assumptions for all three: 16:9 at 1920x1080, 20 to 30 s, music plus click sounds from free local
sources (no paid HeyGen voice or avatar), no voiceover. Real screens come from a demo mode in Lega
with sample data, so no private file names appear.

## A. The red number (recommended)

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take.
The viewer sees a red "6.6 GB free" in the menu bar while they work, the old way of digging
through Finder folders failing, then one click in Lega clearing it while Comet stays open.
- First 2 s: the macOS menu bar at full size, the Lega icon, "6.6 GB" pulsing red; the cursor drifts toward it.
- Beats: hook (red number) → tension (Library folders, a wall of cache names) → reveal (Lega panel opens) → proof (Clean, rows clear one by one, Comet skipped, Advanced asks before deleting) → payoff (the ring drains to teal, 19.6 GB) → claim "One button, room to breathe." → end card (icon, Lega, Download for Mac).
- Look: the page's sage ground, Host Grotesk, the app's teal, real macOS chrome.
- Sound: a light, warm beat; soft clicks on each cleared row.

## B. The number that grows

Device: a number lands. Form: one frame that rebuilds.
A giant "6.6 GB" fills the frame; each cache appears as a tile pulled out of the number and
dissolved, and the number grows as the tiles leave, until "19.6 GB" settles.
- First 2 s: "6.6 GB free" set huge in red on sage, a cursor clicks the period.
- Beats: hook (huge red number) → tension (tiles of cache names crowd the number) → reveal (Lega) → proof (tiles cleared, one stays: Comet, open) → payoff (number grows to 19.6 GB, turns teal) → claim → end card.
- Look: kinetic type only, sage and teal colour fields, app icons on the tiles.
- Sound: a tight rhythmic track, every tile cut on the beat.

## C. Inside the ring (bold)

Device: the object is the explanation. Form: an endless zoom.
The camera dives into the disk ring in Lega's panel and finds layers of clutter inside it,
browser caches, old Codex versions, build leftovers; each layer clears and the camera dives again,
then pulls all the way back out to the menu bar.
- First 2 s: the red ring in Lega's panel fills the screen; the camera starts to fall into it.
- Beats: hook (falling into the ring) → tension (layers of junk) → reveal (each layer named and cleared) → proof (Comet layer stays untouched) → payoff (pull back, ring teal) → claim → end card.
- Look: depth and scale, real app icons as the objects in each layer, teal light.
- Sound: an airy pad that rises as each layer clears.
