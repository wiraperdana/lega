FAQ

What does the main button remove?

Only temporary files that apps make again by themselves, from your browsers, Zoom, developer tools and old app updates.

What if the app is open?

Lega skips it, so you never have to close anything or restart your Mac first.

Can I lose my files?

Your own files only ever go to the Trash, so you can take them back. Anything big waits for your yes.

Why does macOS block it on first launch?

Lega is not signed with an Apple Developer ID yet. Open System Settings, Privacy & Security, then click Open Anyway. Only once.

Which Macs are supported?

macOS 15 Sequoia and later, on Apple Silicon and Intel.

How much does it cost?

Free. Anyone can read and check its code on GitHub.
